# Session review — 2026-09-15

Written for reading cold. Everything below is committed and pushed; every claim
points at the artifact or ADR behind it.

## The headline

**B6's clause (3) was never a prompt problem.** Three prompt conditions were
written, measured and reverted chasing it. A model ladder found it in one run:
`qwen3:4b` and `qwen3:14b` classify the opinions correctly, on the *exact two
assertions* `qwen3:8b` mis-files — and every previous B6 run in this project's
history used `qwen3:8b`.

**The obstacle has inverted.** No judge tested produces all three clauses.
Every working one returns exactly two, and which two depends on the model.

| judge | (1) supported | (2) contradicted | (3) `not_checkable` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qwen3:0.6b` / `1.7b` | 1 claim — below the floor | — | — |
| `qwen3:4b` | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ 2 |
| `qwen3.5:4b` | ✅ | ✅ 4 | ❌ |
| `qwen3:8b` warm | ✅ | ✅ 3 | ❌ |
| `qwen3:8b` cold | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| `qwen3:14b` both states | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ 4 |
| `qwen3:32b-16k` | unparseable, twice | — | — |

**Generation moves this more than size.** `qwen3:4b` and `qwen3.5:4b` — identical
parameter counts — return *opposite* pairs. And the newer generation needed
**five times the budget**: it failed at 6,000 with 23,149 characters of thinking
and no answer.

## Measurements that will outlive the session

**The judge is deterministic within a model load state, not across one.**
Seventeen re-judgements of one transcript gave exactly two outputs; a
pre-registered cold/warm test hit both predictions to the byte. A **cold judge
silently under-reports** — 11 claims and zero contradictions against 20 and three.
This retired the N=5 gate protocol in favour of two draws per state, which
enumerates the space instead of sampling it.

**Position bias: con won 9 of 9 mirror runs**, by 3–9 points, mean +5.0, never
inverting. p ≈ 0.004 — the first result here that clears a significance test.
Same team file both sides, so model and stance are controlled by construction.
It does *not* separate the label from the speaking slot; the experiment that
would is specified in `probe/mirror/README.md`.

**The rubric reaches 45 of 100.** A deliberately weak debater scored 45 against
89, failing per-dimension exactly where the persona was built to fail. That
**falsified** the previous day's "compressed scale" inference: the 85–100 band
was narrow because those debates were competent. What survives is poor resolution
*at the top*, which is where the 100/100 draw sits.

**Malformed JSON runs at roughly a third of calls** — 2 of 6 in one batch, plus
others since. One retry has sufficed once and failed once.

## Where B6 stands

**Still unmet**, and for a new reason: no single model produces all three clauses.
The live option is ADR-024 §2 implemented as *schema* rather than prose — a
`factual` boolean in the claim shape, so the model answers the gating question in
a field instead of holding it in its head. That needs an ADR and a
`schema_version` decision. No prompt wording can make one model do what another
does, which is what today ruled out.

## Machine notes

- 34 GB disk free; both deepseek models removed (they shared one blob, so
  removing only one would have freed nothing).
- **Ollama's default contexts are a hazard.** `qwen3:4b` was given 262,144 tokens
  (43 GB resident, CPU spill); `deepseek-r1:32b` 131,072 (55 GB, OOM kill that
  killed an unrelated process). Cap `num_ctx` before running anything large;
  `qwen3:32b-16k` and `qwen3.5:4b-40k` exist as capped variants.
- Check `ollama ps` for `SIZE` and `CONTEXT` after loading and before committing
  to a run. Disk size says nothing about resident size.
